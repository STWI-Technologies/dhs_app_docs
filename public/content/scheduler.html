Scheduler

Overview

The Scheduler is the calendar for everything that has a date: jobs and appointments, side by side, coloured by the crew doing them. It is where the week gets planned and where it gets rearranged when the day falls apart.

From here you can:

  • See the work by day, week, month or as a plain list.
  • Create a job or an appointment by clicking the slot you want it in.
  • Move work by dragging it to a new time.
  • Respect crew availability, or deliberately override it.
  • Narrow to the crews you care about.

To open it, go to Menu > Scheduler.

Getting around

The header carries the same three controls in every view. Today jumps back to the current date, the arrows step one day, week or month at a time, and the date picker goes straight to a date.

On the right, a segmented control switches the view.

ViewWhat it gives you
DayOne day, split into a column per crew, hour by hour. The view for dispatching.
WeekSeven days at once. The view for planning.
MonthThe whole month, for the shape of it rather than the detail.
ListNo grid at all: the work written out in order, for a date range you choose.

The view it opens on is yours to set in Settings > Dashboard > Default Scheduler Views, under Scheduler Page. The scheduler embedded in the dashboard has its own setting, so the two can differ. In List view the arrows disappear, because the date picker is giving you a range rather than a single date.

Arriving here from a job or an appointment is a special case: the scheduler opens in Week view, on that record's date.

Day view, one column per crew

Day is the only view that splits by crew. Each crew gets a column, titled with its name, and work lands in the column of the crew it is assigned to. Anything with no crew goes into a column called Unassigned.

This is also the only view where a drag can be refused for the wrong reason: dragging a card out of its own crew's column is not a reassignment, and the scheduler stops it with Cannot drop event in different crew column. To change who is doing the work, change the crew on the job or appointment itself.

What a card tells you

Each card carries an icon for what it is, a hammer for a job and a calendar for an appointment, and takes the colour of its crew. Work with no crew uses a neutral colour instead. Recurring jobs are marked as such.

Clicking a card opens a popover with the essentials: who it is for, where, when, which crew and what status. From the popover you open the full record.

Cards cannot be resized. Dragging an edge does nothing, because a duration is not something the calendar lets you invent: change it on the job or the appointment, where the dates belong.

Creating from the calendar

Two buttons at the top right, New Appointment and New Job, start from an empty form.

Clicking an empty slot is quicker. It asks Create New Event and offers the same two choices, with the date and the time you clicked already filled in. In Day and Week views that means date and time; in Month view, just the date.

Moving work by dragging

Drag a card to a new slot and the scheduler asks you to confirm before anything is saved. The confirmation is the reschedule panel, with the new start and end already in it, so you can adjust them before committing.

A drop is refused in two cases:

What you seeWhy
Cannot schedule during unavailable timesThe crew is not available then, and Override Availability is off.
Cannot drop event in different crew columnIn Day view, you dragged the card into another crew's column.

Availability, and overriding it

The calendar knows when each crew is available, from Settings > Availability and from the dates a crew has been marked unavailable. Those hours and days are shaded, and work will not drop into them.

The Override Availability switch in the header turns that guard off for the session, for the times you have to schedule anyway: the emergency job, the Saturday somebody agreed to cover. It changes nothing about the crew's availability, only whether the calendar argues with you. While the calendar is working out what is available you will see Updating availability... in the corner.

Filtering by crew

The crew selector takes as many crews as you want and shows All Crews when you have picked none. It narrows every view, Day columns included. On a Solo plan there are no crews, so the selector is not there and Day view does not split into columns.

When a day is empty

List view says No events for this date rather than showing you an empty grid. The grid views simply show the day with nothing in it, which is the answer you were looking for.

The same work shows up in three places, and which one you want depends on the question. The Jobs and Appointments lists are for filtering and bulk work, the scheduler is for time, and the Map is for where everything is.
