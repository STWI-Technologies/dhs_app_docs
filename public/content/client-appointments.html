Overview

An appointment is a visit rather than the work. Someone comes to look, measure, diagnose or talk it through, and what happens next depends on what they find.

Appointments usually come out of a service request, when the provider needs to see the place before they can put a price on anything.

Not every visit is free, and not every visit is charged. The Total column is how you tell which this one is before anybody arrives.

Inside an appointment

  • Overview: the property, the crew, the scheduled start and end, how long it is expected to take, and whether it is marked as a priority.
  • Services and Products: what is planned for the visit, with quantities and prices.
  • Billing: subtotal, discount, tax and total, when there is something to pay.
  • Messages: the conversation about this visit.

Confirming or cancelling

An appointment can be confirmed, which tells the provider the time works for you, or cancelled when it does not.

Cancelling in the portal is better than not being in. It reaches the crew before they set off, and the record says the visit was called off rather than leaving it looking like nobody was home.

What comes after a visit

Once they have seen the place, the provider turns the appointment into whatever fits: an estimate if you need a price, a job if the work is agreed, or an invoice when the visit itself was the service, such as an inspection or a callout.

Where appointments connect

  • Request Service: most appointments start as a request from you.
  • Estimates: the usual next step after a look.
  • Jobs: when the visit settles what needs doing.
  • Inbox: every appointment has its own conversation.
