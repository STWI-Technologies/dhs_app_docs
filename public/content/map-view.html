Map

Overview

The Map answers the question the lists cannot: where is all of this. Every job and appointment that has a property with an address shows up as a pin, so you can see a day laid out geographically before you commit a crew to it.

It is a read-only view. Nothing is scheduled or edited from the map; it takes you to the record that is.

To open it, go to Menu > Map.

Jobs or appointments

A segmented control at the top left switches between Jobs and Appointments, with the number of matches inside each tab. Both run under the same filters, so each tab tells you its own count whether or not you are looking at it. A tab with nothing in it shows no number rather than a zero.

Narrowing what is on the map

ControlWhat it does
SearchFinds by name and client.
Date rangeOpens on Today. There is no "all time" option here on purpose: a map of every job you have ever done is not useful.
CrewOne crew at a time. Not shown on a Solo plan.
RecenterPuts the map back how it opened, after you have panned and zoomed around.

Reading the pins

Pins that sit close together are grouped into one, with a count on it. Zooming in splits them apart.

Clicking a single pin opens a card for that record: a photo of the property if there is one, the property address, the client, the name of the job or appointment with its type icon, the crew with its colour dot, and the start and end dates. At the bottom, View Job or View Appointment opens the record.

When no photo has been added, the card falls back on its own: Street View for the address first, then a plain map of it, and if neither is available, No location preview available.

Clicking a group opens the list of what is there instead. Picking one shows its card, and a link at the top of the card reading 3 items at this location takes you back to the group. This is how a property with several jobs on the same day is read.

When the map comes up empty

A banner above the map tells you which kind of empty it is, which is the difference between nothing scheduled and nothing matching.

BannerWhat it means
No jobs scheduled for todayYou have not narrowed anything. There is genuinely nothing on this date.
No jobs match "kitchen"Your search found nothing.
No jobs match these filtersThe date range or the crew is excluding everything.
No jobs match your search and filtersBoth at once.

The filters you have applied are listed beside the banner as chips, so you can see what is in the way. With more than one thing applied, Clear all appears to drop them in one go. With only one, its own dropdown already clears it.

The appointments tab says the same four things about appointments.

Arriving from somewhere else

Opening the map from a job or a property brings its filters with it: the map lands on that one record, or on that one property, with the other filters cleared and a breadcrumb back to where you came from. It is the fastest way to answer "where exactly is this" without searching for it.

A pin needs an address

The map plots properties, not clients. A job whose property has no usable address cannot be placed, and will be missing from the map while still being perfectly visible in the Jobs list. If something you expected is not there, check the property on the client record first.
