Crews Management

Overview

A crew is a team of users who work together. Instead of assigning technicians one by one to every appointment, job, or estimate, you group them into a crew and assign the whole team at once. Each crew has a name, a color that identifies it across the platform, and a crew lead who is the primary point of contact for that team.

From this section you can:

  • Create crews with a name, color, description, and members.
  • Edit a crew to change its details, add or remove members, or hand the lead role to someone else.
  • Delete crews you no longer need.
  • Review availability for each crew, and jump to the Availability page to change its schedule.
  • See customer feedback and ratings for each crew.
  • Search and filter crews by name, or by the people on them.

Important: a crew can only be deleted if it is not assigned to any job or estimate. While a crew is in use its delete button stays greyed out, and hovering it explains why: "Cannot delete crew assigned to jobs or estimates."

The Crews list

The Crews list is where you manage every crew. To open it, go to Menu > Crews.

Two things sit above and inside the list:

  • Add Crew — the button at the top right of the page. It opens the create panel.
  • The card header — holds the title with a count of your crews, a search box, and a Users filter.

On a phone the layout folds: Add Crew moves to its own strip at the top of the screen and the search box sits below it, full width.

Sorting

Click the Crew column header to sort the list by name, and click it again to reverse the order. The other columns aren't sortable.

Pagination

The list shows 10 crews per page. Use the picker at the bottom left to switch to 20, 50, or 100, and the controls at the bottom right to move between pages. Changing the search term or the filter takes you back to the first page.

When nothing is listed

Which message you get depends on why the list is empty:

  • A search with no match tells you which keyword came up empty, so you can try another one.
  • A filter with no match suggests clearing your filters.
  • No crews at all explains what crews are for, with a shortcut to this help article.

The guided tour

The first time you open the Crews list, a short tour walks you through the page: the Add Crew button, the list itself, search, the Users filter, and the row actions. Use Next to step through it, or Skip to dismiss it — you'll then be asked whether you want to skip just this tour or turn tours off everywhere. Once finished or skipped, it doesn't come back.

Creating a crew

Click Add Crew. A panel slides in from the right, titled Add New Crew.

Fields

FieldRequiredNotes
Crew NameYesWhat the team is called — "Plumbing Crew", "Emergency Repair Team".
ColorNoThe swatch beside the name. Click it and pick from ten colors; the crew starts out blue. This color is how you recognise the crew in lists and on the scheduler.
DescriptionNoWhat the crew does, where it works, anything worth noting. Several lines fit.
Crew MembersYesAt least one person. Pick them from the dropdown; they're listed in the table underneath.

Adding members

Open the Add Crew Member dropdown and tick everyone who belongs on the crew.

Not everyone on your account shows up here. The list holds users with the Owner, Admin, or Crew role — the people who do field work. Support users and clients are never crew members, and anyone whose account is disabled is left out too. If someone you expect is missing, check their role on the Users page.

Need someone who isn't a user yet? Use the Quick Add shortcut inside the dropdown to create them without leaving the crew form. The new person is added to your account and put straight onto the crew.

Everyone you pick appears in the members table:

  • Crew Lead — the first person you add becomes the lead. To hand the role to someone else, select their radio button.
  • Remove — the trash button takes someone off the crew. It's disabled on the last remaining member, since a crew can't be empty. Remove the current lead and the next member on the list takes over.

Click Add Crew at the bottom of the panel to finish.

Editing a crew

Click the pencil on the crew's row, or click its name. The panel opens titled Edit Crew, with the same fields you filled in when you created it, plus two sections that only make sense once the crew exists.

Members

Add and remove members, or move the lead, exactly as you would when creating a crew.

If someone's account has been disabled they stay in the table, greyed out and marked (Disabled), so you can still see who used to be on the team. They can't be made lead, and they don't count towards the members shown in the list.

Customer feedback

When clients have rated this crew's work, a feedback section shows the average rating and the most recent comment. If there's no feedback yet, the section isn't there at all.

Availability

Below that is a read-only summary of when the crew works.

  • General schedule — the crew's working hours for each day of the week. Consecutive days with the same hours are grouped into one line, and days the crew doesn't work are marked Unavailable.
  • Exceptions — any period the crew is unavailable, whether that's a date range, a whole month, or a season. This part only appears when exceptions exist.

To change any of it, click Manage Availability Settings. That takes you to the Availability page with this crew already selected. If nothing has been set up yet, the summary says so.

Saving

Click Apply Changes. The button stays greyed out until you actually change something, so there's no way to save an untouched form. Cancel closes the panel and keeps the crew as it was.

Deleting a crew

  1. Click the trash button on the crew's row.
  2. Confirm when you're asked whether you're sure.

Deleting is permanent. Crews can't be archived or restored. Records that already reference the crew — past jobs, appointments — keep the crew's details.

If the crew is assigned to jobs or estimates, the trash button is greyed out and hovering it reads: "Cannot delete crew assigned to jobs or estimates." Reassign the crew on that work first, then delete it.

Quick Add

When you're in the middle of something else and need a crew immediately, use Quick Add in the top bar and choose Crew. It also turns up wherever you pick a crew — assigning one to an appointment, for instance.

The short form asks only for a crew name and its members; everything else can wait. As with the full form, the first member you add becomes the lead, and you can create a new user from inside the member dropdown.

Once it's saved you're offered a View Crew shortcut that opens the new crew for editing, so you can fill in its color, description, and schedule.

Until you do, the crew is flagged as incomplete in the Crews list: a marker next to its name offers Complete Info, which opens the same panel. Saving the crew from the full form clears the marker.

Searching and filtering

Search matches crew names. Type in the box in the card header and the list narrows on its own after a moment; capitalisation doesn't matter.

The Users filter is a multi-select of the people on your account. Pick one or more and you'll see only the crews they belong to — the quickest way to answer "which crews is this person on?". Search and the filter work together, and the crew count in the header always reflects what you're looking at.

Crews elsewhere in the platform

Crews are used throughout DHS:

Appointments

When you create or edit an appointment you can assign a crew to it, choosing from a dropdown that shows each crew with its color, or quick-adding one that doesn't exist yet. The crew's availability is taken into account when the appointment is scheduled: if it falls outside their hours or during an exception, you're warned.

Jobs

Crews can be assigned to jobs, and it's these assignments that decide whether a crew can be deleted. The platform can also spot schedule conflicts and warn you before double-booking a crew that's already working during that window.

Estimates

Estimates can carry a crew too. When an estimate comes from an existing appointment or job, the crew is filled in from that record.

Availability

Each crew can keep its own schedule — working days, hours, and exceptions — separate from your business hours. See the Availability Management article for how to set one up.

Dashboard and reports

Crew data feeds the reporting areas: average customer rating per crew, satisfaction trends, and how complaints are distributed across crews.
