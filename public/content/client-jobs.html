Overview

A job is the work itself, booked and being done. Most jobs arrive here because you approved an estimate; some are booked directly when the work was clear enough not to need quoting.

This is where you follow what is happening and, once it is finished, say what you thought of it.

The statuses follow the day: booked, confirmed, the crew on their way, on site, working, paused, and finally completed. You do not have to do anything to move them along; the crew does that from their phones, and the list is how you see it without ringing to ask.

Inside a job

  • Overview: the title, the property, the crew, the start and end dates, and whether a deposit was required.
  • Services and Products: everything being done and supplied, each with its rate or price.
  • Estimates: the quote this job came from, if it came from one.
  • Invoices and Payments: the bills raised from this job. Each one expands to show the payments against it, so you can see what is left rather than only what it cost.
  • Attachments: files the provider has attached, with Download All when there are several. Specifications, installation notes, safety paperwork, manuals.
  • Recurring Schedule: for work that repeats, the pattern it runs on.
  • Billing information: who is being billed and where.
  • Chat: the conversation about this job, kept with it.

Leaving feedback

Once a job is complete you can rate it. The portal asks how satisfied you were with the work and lets you add a comment, then confirm.

It is worth doing. That rating feeds the crew's record on the provider's side, so it reaches the people who did the work rather than disappearing into an inbox.

Where jobs connect

  • Estimates: an approved estimate becomes a job, with everything carried across.
  • Invoices: the bill is raised from the job, which is why the lines match.
  • Appointments: a visit can turn into a job once it is clear what is needed.
  • Inbox: every job has its own conversation.
