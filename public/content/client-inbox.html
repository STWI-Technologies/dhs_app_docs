Overview

The Inbox is every conversation you have with your providers, in one place. It exists so that "when are you coming?" and "is the side gate unlocked?" live with the record they are about, instead of scattered across text messages.

How conversations are organised

A conversation belongs to something. When you start one you choose the service provider and then the chat type, which is what it is about:

  • an appointment
  • an estimate
  • a job
  • an invoice

That is why each record in the portal has its own chat: it is the same conversation, reached from the thing it concerns. Opening the chat on an invoice and finding that thread in the Inbox are two routes to one place.

Starting one

New Chat asks for the provider and the type. If a conversation of that kind already exists it tells you, and takes you to it rather than making a second one.

You can also search your providers from here when you work with several.

Messages

Type in the box and send. You can also attach a file: photographs of the problem, a document, a PDF. The portal shows a preview of documents and PDFs in the thread, so nobody has to download something to see what it is.

There is a length limit on a single message, and the portal tells you when you have gone past it instead of failing to send.

Jumping to the record

A conversation carries links to what it is about: View Estimate, View Invoice, View Job, or the provider's own profile. It saves going back to a list to find the thing you are discussing.

Archiving

Archive takes a finished conversation out of your main list without deleting it. Archived threads have their own view and can be restored.

Archiving is the right move for a job that finished months ago. It keeps the record and gets it out of the way.

When it is empty

A new account has no conversations, and the Inbox says so and invites you to start one. Picking a person shows their threads.

Where the Inbox connects

  • Estimates, Jobs, Invoices, Appointments: each record has the same conversation on it.
  • Dashboard: recent messages appear there so they are not missed.
  • Service Providers: you can start a conversation from a provider's record.
