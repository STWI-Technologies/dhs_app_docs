Overview

Settings is where your own details live: who you are, where your properties are, when you can be visited, and what the portal is allowed to send you.

It is not in the main menu. Click your name and picture in the top right corner, and you get Profile and Settings. Profile goes straight to the profile section inside Settings; Settings opens the list of sections.

Profile

Your name, phone number and email address. These are what a crew has in front of them when they are outside your house, so a wrong number costs more here than in most forms.

The portal checks as you type: a name that is not a name, an address that is not an email, a phone number that is not a real number. It tells you before you save, not after.

Properties

A property is a place work happens at. You can have as many as you own, and every request, appointment, job and invoice is against one of them.

Add one, edit one, and keep them accurate. The address is where crews are sent. If a property is missing from the list when you are making a request, this is where you add it, and the request form will then offer it.

Availability

The days and hours you can be visited. Providers see this when they schedule, so filling it in truthfully is the difference between being offered a time that works and having to reschedule.

This is your availability, not theirs. A provider's own working hours are separate.

Notifications

What you get told about and through which channel. Worth a look once: the useful setting is usually the one that tells you an estimate is waiting, so a quote does not expire unanswered.

General

Portal preferences, including what appears on your dashboard. If your dashboard is showing you something you do not care about, this is where to change it rather than learning to ignore it.

Privacy

What is collected about how you use the portal, and your choice about that. It sits on its own because it is not a preference about the work; it is about you.

A note on who owns your details

Once you are using the portal, your details are yours. Your name, phone, email and properties become read only for the service provider, and their app tells them so. That is the point of the portal: one version of your address, maintained by the person who knows it.

The practical consequence is that if something is wrong, asking the provider to fix it will not work. Change it here.
