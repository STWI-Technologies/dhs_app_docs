Working Offline on the Mobile App

Overview

Basements, crawl spaces, rural properties, parking garages: plenty of the places you work have no signal. The mobile app is built for that. It keeps a copy of your account on the device, so the day carries on when the bars disappear, and it sends everything up when you are back on a connection.

Nothing to switch on. Offline mode is on by default, and the first sign-in is what stocks the device.

Things to know

  • The first sign-in has to finish. It downloads your data and shows Setting up your workspace. Leave before it finishes and the app tells you so: Setup incomplete — offline, and that "Some data hasn't finished downloading, so parts of the app may be unavailable offline."
  • You keep working normally. Start jobs, run timers, add services and products, complete checklists, create clients. Anything you change is saved on the device and marked Unsynced.
  • Nothing to press to send it. "Your queued changes will upload automatically when you reconnect."
  • Three things need a connection, always: attachments, notes and the conversation. They are not kept on the device at all.
  • Two users can change the same record. When that happens the app stops and asks you, rather than picking a winner. See When two users changed the same thing, below.

Before you lose signal

  • Open the app while you still have bars and let the blue Refreshing your data banner run to the end and disappear. That is the whole preparation.
  • Pull down any document you will need on site. Attachments are the one thing the device cannot give you later: a spec sheet, a signed agreement, a photo of the last visit. Open it before you drive out.
  • Check what is still waiting before you leave a site if you are going somewhere with no signal for a long stretch. The offline banner counts your queued changes for you.

What it looks like in the field

A basement with no bars. You arrive, start the job, run the timer, add the two parts you fitted and tick the checklist. The banner says You're working offline and the records you touched pick up a small cloud-off icon. You drive to the next address, the phone finds signal on the way, and everything goes up without you doing anything.

A rural property. The client asks for a price while you are there. You build the estimate on the spot with your own services and products, prices and tax included, because the catalogue is on the device. It reaches the office when you do.

A parking garage. You need the signed service agreement from the client's record. It is an attachment, so it is not there. That is the one to plan for.

The banner that tells you where you stand

There is no permanent status widget to go looking for. A banner appears at the top of the screen when there is something worth telling you, and it leaves when everything is synced and nothing is waiting. No banner means you are up to date.

The full banner lives on your dashboard. List screens show the same card in two lighter forms: the offline one, and a small pill while that particular list is pulling or pushing. Detail and form screens show a thin orange strip that says You're offline, and nothing more.

What it saysWhat is happening

Refreshing your data, the same blue cardEvery later pull. Different title, same card, same tap to expand. The first run gets its own wording because that is the one that decides what you will have offline.
Sending your changesYour queued work is going up. The arrow in the badge points up instead of down, and the detail reads Device → Server.
You're working offline, in orange, with an Offline chipNo connection. With work waiting it adds the count: "1 change · Your queued changes will upload automatically when you reconnect." With nothing waiting, the title stands alone.
Setup incomplete — offline, also orangeYou lost the connection before that first download finished: "Some data hasn't finished downloading, so parts of the app may be unavailable offline." Worth getting back on signal before you rely on the device.

Records themselves carry a quieter mark: a small cloud-off icon on the row of anything you changed offline, and a temporary code (JOB-L1, and so on) on anything you created offline, until the server hands it the real one.

When two users changed the same thing

If you edit a job offline and someone in the office edits the same job, the app cannot know which one is right, so it stops instead of guessing. The banner turns red and reads Sync paused — conflicts found, with how many items "were changed both here and on the server. Pick which version to keep." and a Resolve button on the right.

That opens a stepper that walks the list one record at a time, counting off in the header ("2 of 5 · 1 resolved") so you can see how far you have left. For each record it tells you why it clashed:

  • It was also changed on the server while you were offline.
  • It was deleted on the server while you were offline, and keeping your version will restore it.
  • A matching record already exists on the server, which usually means the same thing got created twice.

Below that, Your version and Server version sit side by side, one row per field that differs. You choose with Keep yours, Keep server, or Merge field-by-field when you want some of each; Delete this record is there too, switched off when another queued change depends on it. Nothing is committed until Finish & resume sync at the end, and nothing else syncs until the list is empty, which is deliberate: it stops a conflict from being buried under later work.

Offline settings

Under Profile > Offline there are three switches. All of them only ever reduce offline activity, so with everything on, the app behaves the way this article describes.

SwitchDefaultWhat turning it off does
Offline modeOnThe app works purely online: nothing is read from or saved to the device, and syncing stops. The other two switches go inert.
Always use latest dataOffOn, the app fetches from the server while you are connected and falls back to the device copy when you are not. Off, it shows the saved copy first, which is faster. Either way your changes are saved online first and only queued when there is no connection.
Background sync and live updatesOnThe app stops syncing in the background and stops listening for live updates from the server.

The same screen has Sync now, for when you want to pull the latest before heading out rather than wait for the app to do it. Offline it tells you plainly: "You're offline. Connect to the internet to sync."

Where offline connects

  • Mobile App: signing in is what stocks the device, so the first sign-in described there is the one that decides what you will have with no signal.
  • Jobs, Estimates and Invoices: the records you open and change on site. Each one you touch offline carries the cloud-off mark until it uploads.
  • Timesheets: the timer you run and the hours you log with no signal land there once you reconnect.
  • Clients and Properties: kept on the device in full, which is what lets you open a client, add a property, or create a new client in a basement.
  • Services and Products: the catalogue, its prices and the tax rates travel with you, which is why an estimate you build offline adds up to the real total.
  • Settings: the three offline switches and Sync now live under your profile, alongside the rest of your preferences.
