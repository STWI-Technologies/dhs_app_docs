Overview

The client portal is your side of the work. It is where you ask for a service, see what was quoted, follow the job while it happens, pay the invoice and talk to the people doing it, without phoning anyone.

You do not sign up on your own. A service provider invites you, and that invitation is what connects your account to theirs.

Your invitation

The provider sends an invitation to your email address. Opening it brings you to the portal with your details already filled in, so all that is left is choosing a password.

If the invitation has gone missing, ask the provider to send it again. They can see whether it is still pending on their side.

Setting your password

The link from the invitation opens Set password. Choose one, confirm it, and you are in. From then on you sign in at the portal with your email address and that password.

Signing in

Go to the portal and enter your email address and password. If you are already signed in on that browser, you land straight on your dashboard.

Magic links

Some messages from a provider carry a direct link to a record, for example an estimate they want you to look at. Tapping it signs you in and opens that record, so you do not have to find it yourself.

If you forget your password

Use Forgot password on the sign in screen. You will get an email with a link that opens Reset password, where you choose a new one. The link is for you alone, so do not forward it.

Your details

The first time you sign in you may be asked to complete your profile: your name, phone number and the property the work happens at. It is worth doing properly. The address is where crews turn up, and the phone number is how they reach you when they are outside.

Once you maintain your own details, they are yours. The provider can no longer edit your name, phone, email or properties. Their app tells them so. If something needs correcting, you do it from Settings.

Finding your way around

The menu holds eight places:

WhereWhat it is for
DashboardWhat is happening right now, at a glance.
Request ServiceAsking a provider for work.
Service ProvidersThe companies you are connected to.
AppointmentsVisits booked with you.
EstimatesQuotes waiting on your answer.
JobsWork that is booked, running or finished.
InvoicesBills and what you have paid.
InboxYour conversations.

Settings is not in that menu. It is behind your name and picture in the top right corner, along with Profile. That is also where you sign out.
