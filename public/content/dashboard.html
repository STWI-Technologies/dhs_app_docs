Dashboard

Overview

The dashboard is what opens when you sign in. It is a single screen that answers four questions without you going anywhere: how the business is doing, what is on the calendar, who is waiting on a reply, and what is still open.

Everything on it is a shortcut. Nothing is edited here: every number, row and card opens the real record when you click it.

The top of the page

Your greeting changes with the time of day and carries your name. To the right of it sit three things, and all three are optional.

ElementWhat it is
ClockThe current time, with the date beside it and seconds inside it if you want them, in 12 or 24 hour format.
TimerThe job timer you have running, if any, so you can stop it from here instead of opening the job.
GearOpens Dashboard Settings, the same controls that live in Settings > Dashboard.

The timer is live. If a crew stops it from the mobile app, or you stop it from the job itself, it disappears from here too.

The four numbers

Four cards across the top of the page, each with a number, a change badge and a small bar chart.

CardWhat it counts
Total JobsHow many jobs the account has.
Total Real ProfitWhat the work you have actually completed has earned.
Pending InvoicesHow many invoices are still waiting to be paid.
Total Potential ProfitWhat is on the table: the work quoted or scheduled but not yet earned.

Money is shortened once it gets long, so $1,240 reads $1.2K and $3,400,000 reads $3.4M. The bar chart is the last eleven periods of that same number, so you can see the shape of it without reading a report.

The badge compares the current period with the one before it. Green with an up arrow means it grew, red with a down arrow means it fell, and a grey 0% means it did not move. When there is nothing to compare against yet, there is no badge at all.

The scheduler card

The calendar sits in the middle of the page, with the same controls as the full page: Today, the arrows, the date picker and the crew filter. Only the views differ: here you get Day, Week and List, and Month is left to the full page.

You can work in it from here. Clicking an empty slot starts a new job or appointment on that date and time, clicking a card opens its popover, and dragging a card reschedules it. View Scheduler at the bottom right opens the full page on the same date.

Which view it opens on is yours to set, separately from the full page, in Dashboard Settings.

The Inbox card

Every conversation with activity, newest first. One row per conversation, not per client, so a client who wrote you from a job and from an estimate shows up twice, each row labelled with where the message came from.

A row carries the chat type, the date and time of the last message, the client, who wrote it and the first line of what they said. A blue circle on the right counts what you have not read. A job whose crew is on the way is tagged EN ROUTE.

Clicking a row opens that conversation in the Inbox. Two settings shape the card: Max List Length caps how many rows it shows, and Show Only New Messages hides everything you have already read.

Upcoming jobs, open estimates, open invoices

Three tabs at the bottom of the page, each with its own count, its own crew filter and a View all link into the full list.

TabColumns
Upcoming JobsName, Scheduled, Crew, Status
Open EstimatesName, Client, Status, Crew
Open InvoicesInvoice, Client, Due Date, Status

The jobs tab has one control the others do not: Upcoming and Today. Upcoming is everything from today forward, Today is only today. How far forward Upcoming reaches is the Upcoming Jobs Window in Dashboard Settings.

Column headers with arrows sort the list. Clicking a row opens the record, and the pencil at the end of the row does the same thing. An estimate that is ready for review is labelled Pending Review here, which is the same status the estimates list calls Ready for Review.

When a tab is empty it tells you why, and offers the way out. No jobs at all offers Add Job; jobs that exist but none coming up says so instead. Open Estimates reads "Anything waiting on a client response appears here", and Open Invoices "Drafts and unpaid invoices appear here".

On a Solo plan there are no crews, so the Crew column and the crew filter are not there.

Tuning what it shows

The gear at the top right and Settings > Dashboard are the same controls, and changing them in one place changes the other. They are listed in full in the Settings article, but the two that make the most difference are Max List Length and Upcoming Jobs Window: on a busy account, a shorter list and a narrower window are the difference between a dashboard that opens instantly and one you wait for.

It keeps itself current

The dashboard listens for changes and refreshes on its own. When someone else completes a job, sends an invoice or replies to a client, you do not have to reload the page to see it.
