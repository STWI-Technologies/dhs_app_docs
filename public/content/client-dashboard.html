Overview

The dashboard is the first thing you see after signing in. It answers one question: what needs me today, and what is coming.

It greets you by name, and the button opposite your name is Request Service, because asking for work is the thing you are most likely to be here for.

The numbers across the top

FigureWhat it counts
Total RequestsEverything you have asked for, across every provider.
Total SpentWhat you have actually paid.
Pending InvoicesBills still waiting on you. The one to watch.
Total Potential SpendingWhat it would come to if you approved everything currently quoted.

That last one is the useful one when several quotes are open at once: it is the difference between deciding on each in isolation and seeing what they add up to.

Open items

One card with three tabs, each showing what is not finished:

  • Upcoming Jobs: work that is booked and has not happened yet.
  • Open Estimates: quotes waiting on your answer.
  • Open Invoices: bills not yet settled.

Anything in these lists opens its full record when you click it.

Inbox

Beside the tabs sits your most recent conversations, so a question from a provider does not sit unread while you look at something else. It opens the full Inbox.

Service providers

Underneath, the companies you are connected to. If you work with more than one, this is the quickest way into a particular provider's record and everything you have with them.

When it is your first day

A brand new account has nothing to show yet, so the dashboard says so plainly and points at Request Service. There is nothing broken about an empty dashboard; it fills up as soon as you ask for something.

Where the dashboard connects

  • Estimates: the Open Estimates tab is the same list, shortened.
  • Invoices: same for Open Invoices.
  • Settings: under General you can change some of what the dashboard shows.
