Overview

An invoice is the bill for work that has been done. It comes from the job, which is why its lines match what you approved, and it stays here with its payments against it so you can see what is settled and what is not.

Total Balance carries two numbers on purpose. A part payment shows as a total with a smaller balance under it, which is the difference between "this is paid" and "this is nearly paid".

Inside an invoice

  • Overview and Job Details: the invoice number, the issue and due dates, the property, and the job it came from. View Job takes you to that job.
  • Services and Products: every line with its SKU, description, quantity or hours, rate and total, then subtotal, discount, tax and total.
  • Payments: each payment with its amount, date, method, reference, who processed it and any notes. Until there is one it says so plainly.
  • Billing information: who is being billed and where.
  • Deposit: whether one was required, or none was.
  • Chat: questions about this bill, kept with it.

Paying

Pay Now takes the payment online.

The button is not always there, and that is not a fault in your account. It only appears when the provider has finished setting up online payments on their side. If you do not see it, that provider is not taking card payments through the portal yet, and you pay them the way you always have. The invoice still tracks what you paid, because they record it themselves.

It can also appear and be unavailable, when there is nothing to pay: an invoice already settled has no balance to take.

Getting a copy

Download Invoice gives you the PDF, for your records, your accountant, or whoever approves spending. If the provider has not generated one, the portal says there is no PDF available rather than handing you an empty file.

Where invoices connect

  • Jobs: the bill is raised from the job, and the job shows it under Invoices and Payments.
  • Estimates: what you approved is what gets billed.
  • Dashboard: Pending Invoices and Total Spent both come from here.
  • Inbox: every invoice has its own conversation, which is the right place to query a line.
