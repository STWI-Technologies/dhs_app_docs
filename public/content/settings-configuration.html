Settings

Overview

Settings is where the account is configured: who you are, when you work, how things are numbered, how you get paid, and what the app tells you about.

It is not in the main Menu. Click your avatar in the top right corner and choose Settings. Your Profile and Billing are in that same popover.

The page has its own sidebar on the left, grouped so that personal preferences and account-wide configuration do not get mixed up:

Each section saves on its own. There is no single save button for the page, so finish one section before moving to the next.

Each section loads in one piece rather than field by field, and every card is read-only until you open it for editing. Leave a card with changes you have not applied and the app stops you: "The changes you made in this section have not been saved. If you continue, they will be lost", with Keep editing and Discard Changes.

Account

The company's own details, in three blocks.

Company Information is the name, contact name and address that identify the business. Billing Information is what appears on the paperwork, when it differs.

Company Name is required. Company Email is not, so a business that would rather be reached on one address can leave it empty. Company email and company phone are checked as you type: if another account already uses them, the field says so before you try to save.

Client Contact Information is the one to get right: the email and phone here are printed in the notification emails your clients receive, so it is how they reach you when they have a question. "At least one client email or phone number is required."

Below that sits your client signup link and client code, both with a copy button. Send the link to a client and signing up through it connects them to your account automatically, with nothing for you to approve afterwards.

Delete Sample Data clears the demonstration records the account was created with. It asks twice, and it cannot be undone. Do it once you have real data and the samples are only getting in the way.

Profile

You, rather than the company: first and last name, email, phone, time zone and language, plus your photo.

Three things are worth knowing here. Changing your email starts a verification, and a banner tells you it is pending. The photo has to be an image under 4 MB. And the language you pick is yours alone, so one person can run the app in Spanish while the rest of the team stays in English.

Your password is changed on this page too.

Notifications

A grid: the kinds of notification down the side, the channels across the top. Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Mobile and Desktop. Tick the combinations you want and save.

If a channel is not available on your account it says so rather than pretending to work. SMS in particular can be switched off account-wide, and the grid shows that instead of silently dropping your messages.

These are your preferences, not the company's. Everyone sets their own.

Security

Login security has one button, Sign out everywhere, which ends every active session. Use it when a phone goes missing.

Known devices lists what has signed in as you: the device, its browser, when it was last seen and from what address. A device you recognise can be marked Trusted, and trusted ones are labelled so the unfamiliar ones stand out.

Privacy

One switch: Share optional product analytics. It is optional, it is off unless you turn it on, and the privacy notice is linked beside it.

Availability

When you can be booked. This is the schedule the rest of the app respects when it offers times.

Start with the schedule type:

  • General Schedule sets the hours for the whole company.
  • Per Crew sets them for one crew, for when a team works different hours from everyone else.

Under it, each day of the week takes one or more time slots, so a business that closes at lunch is two slots rather than one long one. Slots cannot overlap and cannot run past midnight into the next day.

A crew's hours have to sit inside the general schedule. If you give a crew a slot outside it, the form says so instead of saving something the booking rules would ignore.

Exceptions

Exceptions are the days the schedule does not apply: holidays, shutdowns, the week everyone is away. Add one with a date range, and mark it Annual if it happens every year, which is what you want for fixed holidays.

Exceptions are also summarised by month and by season, so you can see the shape of the year rather than a flat list of dates.

Timesheet

One decision, and it saves a lot of manual work: automatic linking.

With it on, a new time entry or a started timer joins the draft timesheet for that week, Monday to Sunday, and if there is no draft the app makes one. With it off, every entry has to be linked by hand.

The main switch is account-wide. Underneath, each person can opt themselves out with Disable automatic linking for myself without changing anything for the rest of the team.

See Timesheets for what linking actually does to an entry.

Business

The account-wide rules, in six blocks, in the order the page lists them.

Counter settings

Estimate ID counter and Invoice number counter set where the numbering continues from, and the current values are shown beside them. This is what you use when you are moving from another system and want your invoice numbers to carry on rather than restart at one.

Invoice Instructions

Text that goes out on every invoice: "Provide instructional text to display on all invoices". One field, with a 512 character counter. There is no longer a default text to fall back on, so whatever you write here is what the client reads, and an empty field means no instructions at all.

Zelle payment details

A block of its own, not part of the instructions. Show Zelle Payment Option on Invoice turns it on or off, and Zelle payment option text is the wording the client sees.

Crew settings

Allow crew to access any job decides how far a crew member can see. Leave it off and they see and log time on the jobs assigned to their crews, and nothing else. Turn it on and any crew member can open and log time on any job in the account.

Privacy settings

Do not share information controls whether your details are shared beyond your own account.

Tax rates

The rates you can put on a line. Add Tax Rate creates one with a name and a percentage, and each row can be edited or removed. These are what the Estimates and Invoices line items pick from.

Accept Payments

Online card payments run through Stripe, and this section is the application and its status.

You apply from here, giving your business type, company name and phone. The section then reflects where the application is: submitted, under review, needing extra verification, or approved with a link into your Stripe dashboard. If Stripe declines, the reason and a reference number are shown so support has something to work with.

This matters beyond the settings page: until Stripe approves you, your clients have no way to pay online. In the client portal the Pay Now button is simply not rendered, so a client who cannot find it is looking at an account that has not been approved yet, not a broken page.

Billing

Your subscription: the plan, the seats and the invoices for the app itself. The section only appears when billing is enabled on the account.

See Plans and Pricing for what each plan includes.

QuickBooks Online

Connects the account to QuickBooks Online so records do not have to be typed twice.

Before connecting, the section explains what syncing does and offers Connect, which sends you to QuickBooks to authorise it. Once connected it splits into three tabs:

  • Connection: status and connected date, the QuickBooks company and its ID, the environment, when it last synced, and the most recent activity. Disconnecting is here too.
  • Sync Settings: what syncs and in which direction, one rule per record type.
  • Sync Activity: the log. Every row shows the type, the direction, when it happened and how it ended: Synced, Failed, Skipped or Conflict. The tab carries a red count when there are errors waiting, and a conflict opens a panel where you choose which version wins.

The record types that cross over are clients, products and services, estimates, invoices, payments and timesheets.
