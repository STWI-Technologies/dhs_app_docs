Overview

Service Providers is the list of companies you are connected to. You get on to it by accepting an invitation from one of them, so the list grows as you take on new providers rather than by searching for them here.

The list

ColumnWhat it shows
Company NameWho they are.
EmailTheir contact address.
PhoneTheir number, for when the portal is not the fastest way.
Service CategoriesWhat they do. This is what tells you which provider to ask.
StatusWhere the connection stands.
ActivityHow recently something happened between you.
Appointments, Estimates, Jobs, InvoicesHow much of each you have with them.

Those four counts are the useful part of the row. They turn "we have used them before" into a number, and they are a link: each one takes you to your records of that kind with that provider.

A provider's record

Opening a provider shows their details and everything you have with them in one place, rather than filtered out of four separate lists.

It is also where you start a conversation with them if you have not got one going already.

Where providers connect

  • Request Service: the first thing the form asks is which provider, and Service Categories here is how you decide.
  • Inbox: conversations are per provider.
  • Invoices: whether you can pay online depends on the provider, not on you. See the Invoices article.
